How it works

From “what do we talk about?” to something real.

Zync is built for the moment two people are already in the same place. Your phone helps find the connection; the conversation stays between you.

1

Build your interest profile

Pick what you love, like or want to try. Add niche interests too — AI can normalize unfamiliar ones for matching.

2

Show or scan a QR

One person shows a Zync QR. The other scans it. No usernames to search and no account exchange required.

3

Reveal hidden matches

Zync compares the two profiles locally and reveals shared interests progressively instead of dumping a list on screen.

4

Start the conversation

Pick a vibe and get a contextual question. No exact match? Zync looks for an interesting crossover instead.

FAQ

The short version.

Do I need an account?

No. Zync V1 is designed without login or registration. Your core profile is local to your device.

Does Zync match me with strangers online?

No. V1 is a face-to-face icebreaker. One person shows a QR and the other scans it while you are together.

What if we have no interests in common?

Zync can use limited interest labels from both profiles to find a plausible crossover and turn it into a question both people can discuss.

Does Zync need AI to work?

No. AI improves custom-interest normalization and conversation prompts, but the app has local fallback questions if the AI service is unavailable.

Where is my data stored?

Your profile, matching history and Interest DNA remain local in V1. Some bounded interest text leaves the device only when an AI-assisted feature is used. See the Privacy Policy for full details.
