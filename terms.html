1. What Zync does

Zync is a face-to-face social icebreaker. It lets people create local interest profiles, exchange selected profile information through QR codes, discover shared interests locally, and use optional AI-assisted features for interest normalization and conversation prompts.

2. Local profile, QR sharing and temporary relay

Your Zync profile is primarily stored on your own device. When you show a Zync QR, you intentionally make the selected information encoded in that QR available to the person who scans it. Only show your QR to people with whom you are comfortable sharing that information.

That response is encrypted on the scanning phone with a one-time key from the QR before it is sent to the relay. Relay sessions are temporary and designed to expire automatically; further details are in the Privacy Policy.
