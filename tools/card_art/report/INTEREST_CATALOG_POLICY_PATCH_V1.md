# Interest Catalog Expansion & Rights Policy Patch V1

This patch acts on the release-readiness audit.

- Adds everyday Social / Lifestyle / Wellness / Creative / Tech / Entertainment-format interests.
- Adds aliases instead of duplicate canonicals for pottery, model making, clubbing, figurines and model cars.
- Adds a major-car-brand affinity family under `transport/cars/brands`.
- Car brands remain matchable but are `licensedOnly` for Cardverse baseline art.
- Named artists/titles/franchises are classified as partner/rightsholder territory.
- Generic hobby/culture concepts remain eligible for original Zync art.
- Adds safe Zync Now support for browsing/thrifting, social exploration, low-pressure wellness resets and podcasts/audio.
- Health-adjacent and alcohol-sensitive areas remain conservative.
- No fal.ai generation is performed by this patch.
