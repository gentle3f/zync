## Localization architecture finding

The app currently has UI ARB files for:

- English (`en`)
- Spanish (`es`)
- French (`fr`)
- Japanese (`ja`)
- Korean (`ko`)
- Portuguese (`pt`)
- Chinese fallback (`zh`)
- Simplified Chinese (`zh-Hans`)
- Traditional Chinese (`zh-Hant`)

But ordinary compact interest rows currently encode only:

`id | category | cluster | rank | en | zh-Hant | zh-Hans | aliases`

`parseInterestFamily()` can optionally carry Japanese and Korean, but there is still no full interest-label path for Spanish, French and Portuguese.

For a USA-first launch, **Spanish is the most important missing interest-localization layer after English**.

Preferred direction: canonical interest taxonomy stays language-neutral; localized labels and aliases are stored per locale rather than adding ever more fixed columns to the row format.
